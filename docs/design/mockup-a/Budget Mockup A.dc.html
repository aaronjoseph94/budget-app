Type, shape, spacing

Body: the system stack already in index.css (ui-sans-serif / SF Pro / Segoe). Caveat and Comfortaa retire; every figure uses font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums (the existing .tnum).

Scale: h1 32/700 −0.02em · card title 18/600 · stat value 32/700 · body 15 · table 15 · hint 14 · label 12–13 muted.

Radius: panel and cards 16 · buttons, inputs 10 · tiles 12 · pills 999. --radius becomes 1rem. Borders 1px --border; no drop shadows on cards (one on the sign-in card only).

Layout: sidebar 248 (rail 72) · main panel margin 12 · content padding 28 · card grid gap 20 · stat grid gap 16 · 44px targets kept everywhere.

Stat cards: white → tint gradient (accent, rose, green, sky) with a 40px icon tile. List cards: 48px icon tile, % pill in the list hue, header row tinted, name cell carries a 3px mini bar, negative Left is a rose pill with white text.

Navigation change (App.tsx, MoreScreen.tsx)

From 1024px the phone bottom bar and the desktop top bar are replaced by one sidebar grouped like More: Plan (Week, Month, Year, Paycheck, Bill calendar since ADR 0014) · Money (Savings, Debts, All transactions) · Coach (Coach, Forecast, Reports) · Inbox (Review with its count, Add) · Setup (Setup, Settings, Help). Ask, the check-in and AI settings open from their parents. Below 1024px the existing phone bar stays; the sidebar collapses to a 72px rail between 768 and 1024.

The Week · Month · Year switch (PeriodSwitch.tsx) stays on those three screens; Paycheck left it for its own screen (ADR 0014). The ? HelpButton stays beside every title. The top bar adds a ⌘K “Search or jump to…” (new) and the primary + Add.
